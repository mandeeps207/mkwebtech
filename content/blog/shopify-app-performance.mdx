## Performance is product quality

The best Shopify apps help merchants sell more without adding operational drag or storefront latency. Theme app extensions, careful script loading, and lean admin screens make the difference.

## A simple checklist

- Render only where the merchant opts in.
- Keep storefront scripts small and deferred.
- Measure preview, publish, and storefront paths independently.
