## Overview

WooCommerce Pre-Orders gives WordPress stores a structured way to sell products before inventory is ready. It is useful for product launches, manufacturing runs, book releases, limited drops, and high-demand restocks.

The plugin keeps preorder details close to the product and order workflows WooCommerce teams already use. Operations teams can see what has been promised, when release windows arrive, and which customers need updates.

## Operational Notes

Preorders work best when payment rules, release timing, and customer communication are explicit. This plugin makes those decisions visible in the admin so teams can launch with fewer manual checks.
