## Overview

Countdown Timer helps Shopify merchants add tasteful urgency to campaigns without custom theme edits. It is built for limited-time drops, flash sales, restocks, preorder windows, holiday campaigns, and evergreen offers.

The app focuses on clear campaign setup: choose where the timer appears, define how the countdown behaves, preview the customer experience, and publish when the launch is ready.

## Campaign Strategy

Strong urgency works best when it is honest and specific. Countdown Timer helps teams communicate real deadlines, shipping cutoffs, launch windows, and limited offer periods without creating a noisy shopping experience.
